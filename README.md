# Blinkit Sales Analysis 

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Blinkit grocery sales data to uncover key business insights related to sales performance, customer preferences, and outlet characteristics. The analysis is performed using Microsoft Excel and Power BI, with interactive dashboards to support data-driven decision-making.

## Objectives
* Analyze the impact of fat content on total sales.
* Identify top-performing item types.
* Compare outlet performance based on fat content.
* Evaluate how outlet establishment year/type affects sales.
* Analyze sales distribution by outlet size.
* Assess geographical sales distribution.
* Provide a comprehensive breakdown of all KPIs by outlet type.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Total Sales: Overall revenue generated from all items sold.
Average Sales: Average revenue per transaction.
Number of Items: Total quantity of items sold.
Average Rating: Average customer rating of products.

## Key Insights
1. Total Sales by Year

Sales peaked in 2018, reaching approximately $205K, representing a 40% increase compared to the previous year. This growth is largely driven by the expansion in the number of outlets, which increased to 1,463 outlets (a 36% year-over-year increase).

2. Outlet Type Analysis
Supermarket Type1 dominates the business with 5,577 outlets, generating approximately $787K in revenue.
Grocery Stores account for 1,083 outlets, contributing around $152K in revenue.
Supermarket Type2 and Type3 have similar outlet counts (928 and 935) and generate comparable revenues ($131K and $130K respectively).

Supermarket Type1 is the primary driver of overall revenue.

3. Item Type Analysis
Fruits & Vegetables: $178K revenue | 1,232 units sold
Snack Foods: $175K revenue | 1,200 units sold
Household: $135K revenue | 910 units sold

These top 3 categories contribute:

40% of total revenue
39% of total items sold
4. Outlet Location Analysis
Tier 1: $336.40K (28%)
Tier 2: $393.15K (32%)
Tier 3: $472.13K (39%)

Tier 3 locations generate the highest sales, indicating strong demand in smaller cities.

5. Item Fat Content Analysis
Low Fat: 64% of total revenue
Regular Fat: 36% of total revenue

Customers show a strong preference for low-fat products, making them a key revenue driver.
